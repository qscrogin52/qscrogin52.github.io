Our Program

Baseball practices designed for young kids.

The goal is not to make practice complicated. Kids need clear instruction, safe equipment, quick activities, and coaches who keep the game fun.

What players learn

  • Throwing basics
  • Catching and tracking the ball
  • Hitting fundamentals
  • Base running
  • Listening, teamwork, and confidence

What to bring

For normal practices, families do not need to bring equipment. Building Blocks Baseball provides the practice gear.

Players should wear comfortable clothes and tennis shoes.

Practice format

Most practices are about 45 minutes and use stations, games, and short skill activities to keep younger players engaged.

Built for schools and childcare centers

We keep the program organized for schools by providing the equipment, schedule structure, communication tools, and parent-facing registration pages.
