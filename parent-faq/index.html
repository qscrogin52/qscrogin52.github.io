Parent FAQ

Common questions, clear answers.

Here are the basics parents usually need before the first practice.

Does my child need to bring equipment?

No. Building Blocks Baseball provides the equipment for normal practices. Players should wear comfortable clothes and tennis shoes.

What ages is this for?

Most programs are designed for preschool and early elementary players, usually around ages 3–6. Exact ages can vary by school.

How long is practice?

Most practices are about 45 minutes unless your school’s schedule says otherwise.

What if registration is closed?

If a school says registration is closed, that session is full or no longer accepting registrations. Check back for the next season or contact us.

Where can I see the schedule?

Open the Schools page, search for your school, and select View Details.
